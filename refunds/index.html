Refund Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

If it's not for you, you get your money back. 14 days, no questions.

How it works

Within 14 days of buying any app or licence from blushore.live (such as LP33 Definitive Edition), email hello@blushore.live with your order number or the email you used to buy. You don't need to give a reason.

The refund is made by Paddle.com, which processed your order, back to the way you paid. It usually shows up within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. Once refunded, the licence key stops being yours to use.

App Store and Google Play

Purchases made inside an app from the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google. Ask them through reportaproblem.apple.com or Google Play's order history.

Questions

Write to hello@blushore.live. See also the Terms.
