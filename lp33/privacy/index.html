LP33 · Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

I made LP33 on my own. It keeps everything on your computer or phone. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Who I am. Bhavya Sehgal, India. I am the person responsible for your data under the GDPR and similar laws. Write to hello@blushore.live; I read every message myself.

What I collect

Nothing through the apps. LP33 has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporter and no account. I never receive your library, what you listen to, or anything that identifies you.

What stays on your device

Your settings, a copy of your library (liked songs, albums, playlists, artists, what you played), pins, lyrics you opened, and your purchase or licence. It is removed when you uninstall LP33 or delete its cache or data in Settings. On the computer you can turn the library off or delete the cache at any time in Settings › Privacy.

What LP33 reads, and why

Your Spotify library (Mac and Windows), only after you allow it. LP33 reads it through the Spotify desktop app on your computer. You never sign in to Spotify in LP33 and it never sees your password. It controls Spotify only when you ask: play, pause, skip, seek, volume, like, save, follow, add to a playlist or the queue.

Apple Music, only if you allow it or sign in with your Apple Account. Your library is read from Apple and the music plays through Apple's player. Apple's privacy policy covers that.

Microphone, only while Snap Control (desktop) is on, or the visualizer is listening (phones). The sound is analysed on your device for a finger snap or for how loud the music is, and is never recorded, saved or sent.

System audio (Mac, Windows), only while the visualizer is open. Measured, never recorded.

Camera (iPhone, Android), only while you scan the pairing code on your computer. The picture is read on the phone and never saved or sent.

Motion (phones), only for the Shake gesture, on the phone.

Notification access (Android, only if you choose "Spotify on this phone"): lets LP33 see the Spotify app's media session to show and control the song. It reads no notifications or messages.

Notifications: only the play controls on the lock screen and notification shade.

What goes online

Album covers and artist photos from Spotify or Apple, when you look at them.

Lyrics: when you open lyrics, the song's title, artist, album and length go to LRCLIB, a free lyrics service.

Updates (Mac and Windows): LP33 downloads a small version file from Google Drive. Nothing about you is sent.

Phone remote: if you pair a phone with LP33 on your computer, the phone talks to your computer over your home network. Away from home, sealed messages pass through my relay, a small service on Cloudflare. It cannot read them and keeps nothing.

These services (Spotify, Apple, LRCLIB, Google, Cloudflare) see your internet address, like any website. They have their own policies. No ads or tracking scripts are loaded from anywhere.

Buying

On the iPhone, LP33 Pro is bought through Apple's App Store, and on Android through Google Play. I learn only that you own it, never your payment details.

For the Mac and Windows app you buy a licence from Lemon Squeezy, who sells it to you as the seller of record (they take the payment, the VAT or sales tax and the card details). Their privacy policy applies to what they collect. They send me your name, email, country and order number so I can send you the licence key and answer support mail. I keep the email and order number as long as the licence exists (so I can re-send or revoke a key), and the legal records of a sale for as long as tax law in India requires. The key contains your email and is checked on your device, with no server involved.

Why I may use your data (GDPR)

Contract (to deliver the licence you bought), legitimate interest (support, preventing key abuse), and legal duty (tax records). No profiling, no sale of data, no ads.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct, delete or export the data I hold about you (email and order number), object to its use, or complain to your data protection authority. Write to hello@blushore.live and I will answer within one month. The apps hold your data on your device only: delete it in Settings or by uninstalling.

Children

LP33 is not made for children under 13 (or under 16 where your law says so) and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here with its date.
