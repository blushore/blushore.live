Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Ownplay plays the media on your own computer on your own screens. I never see it. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Who I am. Bhavya Sehgal, India. I am the person responsible for your data under the GDPR and similar laws. Write to hello@blushore.live; I read every message myself.

What I collect

Nothing through the apps. Ownplay has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporter and no account. I never receive your movies, shows, photos, files, what you watch, or anything that identifies you.

Where your media goes

Only between your computer running Ownplay and the devices you pair with it by scanning its code. The connection is end-to-end encrypted. At home it stays on your network. Away from home your devices find your computer through a public directory that nobody owns, and when a direct connection isn't possible, encrypted traffic passes through a public relay. Relays can't read it and keep nothing. You can turn both off in the Mac app's Settings.

What stays on your devices

Your library and its details, posters, watch progress, settings and pairing keys. Remove them by revoking a device on the computer, by forgetting the computer in the app, or by uninstalling.

What the apps use, and why

Camera (iPhone, iPad, Android), only while you scan the pairing code. The picture is read on the device and never saved or sent.

Local network, to find and talk to your computer at home.

Photos (iPhone, Android), only if you turn on Photo Backup. Your photos and videos are copied to your own computer, never to me or anyone else.

What goes online

Movie and show details: your computer looks up titles to find posters and descriptions, at TMDB with your own TMDB key, or at Wikipedia and Wikidata. Only titles and years are sent, never your files. Cast photos on title pages load straight from TMDB's image servers.

These services see your internet address, like any website. They have their own policies.

Buying

The phone and TV apps are free. For the computer app you buy a licence from Paddle, who sells it to you as the seller of record (they take the payment, tax and card details). Their privacy policy applies to what they collect. They send me your name, email, country and order number so I can send your licence key and answer support mail. I keep the email and order number as long as the licence exists, and the legal records of a sale for as long as tax law in India requires. The key is checked on your computer, with no server involved.

Why I may use your data (GDPR)

Contract (to deliver the licence you bought), legitimate interest (support, preventing key abuse), and legal duty (tax records). No profiling, no sale of data, no ads.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct, delete or export the data I hold about you (email and order number), object to its use, or complain to your data protection authority. Write to hello@blushore.live and I will answer within one month.

Children

Ownplay is not made for children under 13 (or under 16 where your law says so) and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll post the new version here with its date.
