Before you ask

Does anything go through a server?

Your devices talk straight to your Mac, encrypted. Nothing passes through a server of mine.

What if my Mac goes to sleep?

The library sleeps with it. Keep the Mac on and awake while you watch.

What happens after two devices?

Devices you have already paired keep working for good. Ownplay Pro lets you add as many as you like.

Does it run on Windows?

Yes, Windows 10 and 11. Auto HDR is Mac only for now. Windows may say it doesn't recognise the app the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway.

How does Pro unlock?

Your licence appears right after you pay, and arrives by email too. Paste it in Ownplay under Settings, General, License. One licence per household, on the computer that runs your library. Lost it? Find my licence.
