Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Yohaku collects nothing. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Yohaku is made by Blu Shore. This policy covers the Yohaku app for Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Yohaku does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Where your things live

Everything you make in Yohaku, including your plans, ink, notes, to-dos, stickers and settings, is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.

Photos you add as stickers are copied into that storage when you pick them with the system photo picker. Yohaku can't see the rest of your photos.

Device backup

If Android's own backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Yohaku's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Android and Google under your account's settings. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Yohaku deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

Yohaku is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
