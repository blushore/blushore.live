Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Your plans stay on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Yohaku is made by Blu Shore. This policy covers the Yohaku app for Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Blu Shore never receives your plans, ink, notes, voice memos, photos or calendar. Yohaku has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own.

Where your things live

Everything you make in Yohaku, including your plans, ink, notes, to-dos, voice memos, stickers and settings, is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage.

Photos you add as stickers are copied into that storage when you pick them with the system photo picker. Yohaku can't see the rest of your photos.

Ink to text (optional)

Ink to text is off until you turn it on. To turn handwriting into typed words, Yohaku uses Google ML Kit. The first time you turn it on, ML Kit downloads a handwriting model from Google over Wi-Fi. Your handwriting is then read on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

ML Kit sends Google limited technical data to run and improve it: device and app information (such as phone model, Android version and Yohaku's version), performance and error data, the language you write in, and an identifier for the installation. Google encrypts it in transit and does not share it with third parties. Google's description. Turn Ink to text off in Settings to stop using ML Kit.

Microphone

Yohaku asks for the microphone the first time you record a voice memo or add a plan by voice. Memos are saved on your phone and transcribed on your phone.

When you add a plan by voice, Yohaku uses your phone's own speech recogniser. If your phone can't recognise speech on the device, Android's speech service (usually Google's) handles it under your phone's settings, as it does for your keyboard's microphone. Yohaku never sends audio anywhere itself.

Calendar

If you turn on calendar sync, Yohaku reads and writes events in the calendars on your phone through Android. It doesn't send them anywhere. Your calendar account (for example Google Calendar) syncs them as it always does.

Notifications

Nudges and the hourglass use notifications on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Device backup

If Android's own backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Yohaku's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is run by Android and Google under your account's settings. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Yohaku deletes all of its data from your device. Events Yohaku added to your calendar stay in that calendar until you delete them there.

Children

Yohaku is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
